Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Edulink ("we", "our", "the app") is a social network for university students. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have, including how to delete your account and data.

Information we collect

Account and profile information

  • Email address and password (used for sign-in through Firebase Authentication), or, if you choose "Continue with Google", the name and email address on your Google account
  • A username that you choose when you sign up, which other users can see
  • Display name, university, academic level and course, which you provide during registration
  • The communities you join
  • Optional extras you choose to add: a bio, a profile picture, a linked GitHub account, and a pinned Spotify song
  • A record of when you accepted our Terms of Use

Content you create

  • Posts, comments and voice-note replies, with any images and voice recordings you upload
  • Messages you send in community Rooms (group chats) and in direct messages
  • Links you share, including links to Spotify, TikTok, Instagram and YouTube
  • Your follows, followers, friends and likes
  • Reports you file about content or people, and the list of people you have blocked

Ranks, Clout and streaks

  • Game ranks and Clout scores shown on your profile. These are assigned by our reviewers or earned through activity in the app.
  • If you link GitHub, your public GitHub activity is used to show your GitHub stats and streak on your profile.

Automatically collected information

  • Basic device and app diagnostics needed to keep the app running reliably
  • A device token, so we can send you push notifications
  • Advertising identifiers, because the app shows ads (see Google AdMob below)

How we use this information

  • To provide the app's features: your account, communities, feed, friends and messaging
  • To show your posts, comments and profile to other users as the app's social features intend (see "Who can see what")
  • To send you notifications, for example when someone follows you, messages you, or comments on your post
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports, removing content that breaks our community rules, and acting on accounts that do
  • To show ads
  • To maintain and improve the app's reliability and security

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Who can see what

  • Posts, comments, your username and your profile can be seen by other Edulink users.
  • Room messages can be seen by the members of that Room.
  • Direct messages can be seen by the people in that conversation. They are stored on our servers and are not end-to-end encrypted. If someone reports a message, a short excerpt is included in the report so we can review it.
  • Reports can be seen only by us.
  • Your block list is private to you.

Links and embedded content

When a post, message or profile contains a link to Spotify, TikTok, Instagram or YouTube, the app asks that platform for a preview of the link. The link we send has tracking parts removed. If you open the player, it loads the platform's own embedded player inside the app. That platform may collect information such as your IP address and device details, under its own privacy policy. We do not control what those platforms collect. If you would rather not load them, simply do not open the links.

Reports, blocking and moderation

You can report posts, comments, messages and people, and block people, from inside the app. Blocking someone hides their content from you and stops them from messaging you. Reports are reviewed by us, and we may remove content or act on accounts that break our community rules. We may keep reports, including who filed them and a short excerpt of the content, for as long as we need them to keep people safe or to meet legal duties.

How long we keep your data

We keep your account information and content for as long as you have an account. You can delete individual posts and comments at any time. When you delete your account, we delete your data as described below. We may keep reports, as explained above.

Your choices and rights

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, directly in the app: Settings → Account actions → Delete account. You will be asked to confirm with your password.

  • Straight away: your account, profile and posts are deleted and you are signed out.
  • Within 30 days: we remove what is left, including your chats, your Room messages, your comments on other people's posts, your follower lists, and the photos and voice notes you uploaded.
  • What may be kept: reports about abuse that involve you, where we need them to keep people safe or to follow the law.

Deleting an account cannot be undone. If you no longer have the app, you can request deletion from our account deletion page or by emailing us at the address below from the email address you signed up with.

Accessing or correcting your data

Most of your information (display name, university, course, bio) can be edited directly in Settings. For anything else, contact us using the details below.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored using industry-standard providers (Google Firebase and Cloudflare) with access controls limiting who can read or modify it. We do not have direct access to your account password; authentication is handled by Firebase.

Children's privacy

Edulink is for university students aged 16 and over. It is not directed at anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
