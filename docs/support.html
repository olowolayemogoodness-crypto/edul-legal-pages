Common questions

I can't sign in.
Check your email and password, or use the "Forgot password" option on the sign-in screen. If it still fails, email us from the address you signed up with.

I'm not in my department community.
Make sure your university, course and level are saved on your profile, then close and reopen the app. If you are still missing, email us your username and course.

I'm not getting notifications.
Open your phone's Settings, find Edulink, and turn on Notifications. Then open Edulink once while signed in.

How do I report or block someone?
Tap the three-dot menu on a post, comment, message or profile and choose Report or Block. We review reports and remove accounts that break our rules.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, open Settings and choose Delete account. You can also read the steps at our account deletion page, or email us.
