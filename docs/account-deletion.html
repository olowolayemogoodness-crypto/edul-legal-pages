Delete your Edulink account

Edulink: the social network for university students

You can ask us to delete your Edulink account and the data that goes with it. There are two ways to do it.

1. In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Edulink and sign in.
  2. Open Settings, then go to Account actions and tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

2. If you can't open the app

Email us a request, and send it from the same email address you signed up to Edulink with, so we can find your account.

Email a deletion request

Subject line: "Delete my Edulink account". Please include your username if you remember it.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, rank and Clout, and your GitHub and Spotify links
  • Your posts, stories, comments and community Room messages
  • Your chats, and your lists of followers and people you follow
  • Your sign-in account
  • Photos and voice notes you uploaded

What may be kept

  • Reports about abuse that involve you, where we need them to keep people safe or to follow the law.

How long it takes

In the app: your account, profile and posts are deleted straight away. Anything left over, such as your messages in other people's chats and files you uploaded, is removed within 30 days.

By email: we finish deleting your account within 30 days of your request.

Deleting an account is permanent and cannot be undone.
